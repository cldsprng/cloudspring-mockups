About the clinic

HugoDerm Skincare Clinic, Roxas Avenue

HugoDerm Skincare Clinic is a skincare and dermatology clinic on the second floor of the Davao Yakal Tire Supply Building at 99 Roxas Avenue, Poblacion, Davao City. The clinic handles both medical skin conditions — psoriasis, keloids, warts, acne — and aesthetic treatments such as facials, peels, fillers and IV glutathione.

For appointments and enquiries, contact the clinic by phone during opening hours, or message the Facebook page. Clinic contact: Karen Pasco.
