There is a second, closer problem. A Philippine directory currently lists the hospital at 737 Aurora Blvd with postcode 1112, while listing its own veterinarians at 704 Aurora Boulevard with postcode 1100 — and then describes the two as half a kilometre apart. An owner following that listing arrives at the wrong door on the right street.
