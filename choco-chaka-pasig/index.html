Why this page exists

A directory has filed you in the wrong city

DirectMap.ph carries a listing for “Choco & Chaka’s Pet Grooming Services & Hotel, Mandaluyong”. There is no Mandaluyong branch. The mistake is not buried in the page body — it is in the web address itself, which is what a search engine reads first.

directmap.ph/mandaluyong/17021  ←  you are in Pasig

A listing that gets your city wrong is not being careful about your Monday closure or your 4:30pm cut-off either. That is the version of you a customer meets at 5pm on a Monday, dog in the car, arriving at a building in the wrong municipality.

This page is the version you write. One address, one set of hours, one rule about Mondays — and you decide what it says.

What a page like this fixes

Four things worth getting right

01

The city, in the URL

Yours will say Pasig, because you wrote it. The directory version says Mandaluyong in the address bar and cannot be argued with.

02

The Monday rule, up front

Closed Mondays and holidays. On the first screen, not four scrolls into a listing that never asked you.

03

A 4:30pm cut-off nobody misses

The most common wasted trip in grooming is arriving after the last accepted slot. It is stated twice on this page.

04

Boarding is the booking you cannot lose

A missed groom costs one slot. A misrouted hotel booking costs several nights and the customer who was going to make them.
