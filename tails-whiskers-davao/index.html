The reason for this page

Mamay is listed. Mamay's buttons go to Ma-a.

Petagon's “Pet Grooming in Davao City: 19 Spots” carries all four of your branches, at positions 15 to 18. On the Mamay entry, the Website button and the Facebook button both open the Ma-a page. Someone who searched, found Mamay, read the Mamay address and tapped through is now looking at a shop on the other side of the city.

The same listing sends every other branch's “Website” button to Facebook too, because there is no website to send them to. That is the whole of the fix: give the four branches one address of their own, and let the directory point at it.
