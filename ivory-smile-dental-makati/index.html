Treatments

Cosmetic and general dentistry

The treatments below are the ones the clinic publicly lists. Suitability and the full plan are confirmed at consultation.

Flagship

Porcelain Emax veneers

Ultra-thin all-ceramic shells, matched for colour and translucency so the result reads as teeth rather than as veneers.

Zirconia & Emax crowns

Full-coverage crowns for teeth that need rebuilding rather than resurfacing, in the same materials family as the veneers.

Braces & orthodontics

Alignment treatment planned in-clinic, with adjustment visits scheduled around your own week.

Cleaning & scaling

Routine oral prophylaxis — the appointment that keeps cosmetic work looking the way it did on day one.

Dental X-rays & consultation

Imaging and assessment in-clinic, so the plan you are quoted is based on what is actually there.

Smile makeovers

Veneers, crowns, whitening and alignment combined into one staged plan rather than sold as separate appointments.

How a veneer case runs

Four visits, explained before you commit

1

Consultation

2

Design

Shade, shape and how many teeth are involved, agreed with you before any preparation begins.

3

Fitting

4
