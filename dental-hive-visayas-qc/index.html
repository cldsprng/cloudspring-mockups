Our services

Straighter, healthier, better-looking teeth — under one roof

Three areas of care: orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry and everyday general dentistry for the whole family.

Orthodontics Braces

Braces treatment to correct crowded, gapped or misaligned teeth, with regular adjustment visits as your smile moves into place.

  • Braces
  • Adjustments
  • Bite alignment

Cosmetic dentistry

Treatments focused on how your smile looks — brightening discoloured teeth and improving the shape and appearance of your front teeth.

  • Teeth whitening
  • Smile makeover

General dentistry

Routine check-ups and the everyday care that keeps problems small — for adults and children alike.

  • Check-ups
  • Consultation

Cleaning

Professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar build-up, followed by advice on keeping your teeth and gums healthy at home.

Fillings

Restoring teeth affected by decay so you can eat and smile comfortably again, with a finish that blends in naturally.

Extractions

Careful removal of a tooth that can no longer be saved, with clear aftercare guidance for a smooth recovery.

About the clinic

A neighbourhood dental clinic on Visayas Avenue

Dental Hive — Visayas Avenue is a dental clinic in Diliman, Quezon City. We look after general dentistry, orthodontics and cosmetic dentistry, which means most of what a family needs — from a routine cleaning to a full course of braces — can be handled in the same place, by the same team.
