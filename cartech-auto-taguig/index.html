Why this page exists

One independent shop, listed between two dealerships and a chain

Checked 23 August 2026. taguig.com/business/auto-repair/ is a free directory page listing seven auto repair businesses in Taguig. Cartech is on it, and so are Ford Global City, Toyota Global City, Rapide and Roadstar The Fort. Same page, same font, same size entry. A driver scanning that list has no way to tell the independent tyre specialist from a dealer service department.

What the Cartech entry there carries: a name, an address and a phone number. What it does not carry: a website, opening hours, a rating, a service list, or any mention of Bridgestone. The one fact most likely to win the job is missing from the only page carrying the name.

The name itself is spread thin as well. Five trading variations run across five different surfaces, and the vymaps page still ranking for one of them returns a 404. There is no cartech.ph, no cartechtaguig.com and no cartechauto.ph. All three were checked on 23 August 2026 and none of them resolves.

None of that is a criticism of the workshop. It is a signposting problem, and one page the shop owns is what fixes it.
