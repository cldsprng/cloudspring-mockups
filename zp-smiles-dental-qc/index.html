Our services

General dentistry for the whole family

Straightforward, everyday dental treatment for patients in Novaliches and along Quirino Highway.

Dental consultation & check-up

A full look at your teeth and gums, an explanation of what we find, and a clear plan for what to do next.

Oral prophylaxis (cleaning)

Professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar build-up, keeping gums healthier and breath fresher.

Tooth fillings

Restoring teeth affected by decay so you can bite and chew comfortably again, with a natural-looking finish.

Tooth extraction

Careful removal of a tooth that can no longer be saved, with clear aftercare advice before you leave.

Preventive care & advice

Practical guidance on brushing, flossing and diet so small problems are caught early instead of getting worse.

Follow-up & recall visits

Scheduled return visits so treatment is completed properly and your teeth are checked regularly.

These are general dentistry services. If you need something that is not listed here, message or call the clinic first and we will let you know whether we can help or refer you.
