Treatments

Treatments tailored to your skin

Every treatment begins with a consultation so Heidi can recommend what genuinely suits your skin and your goals.

Wrinkle reduction

Injectable treatment for expression lines, planned around how your face naturally moves.

Rejuran

Skin-conditioning injectable treatment aimed at overall skin quality and texture.

Bio-remodelling

Hydrating injectable treatment designed to support firmness and glow rather than add volume.

PDO threads

Thread treatment used to support and lift specific areas, discussed in detail at consultation.

Micro-needling

Collagen-stimulating treatment for texture, scarring and general skin renewal.

IPL

Intense pulsed light for pigmentation, redness and other light-responsive skin concerns.

RF tightening

Radiofrequency treatment used to encourage firmer-looking skin over a course of sessions.

Tattoo removal

Laser tattoo removal, planned session by session according to the ink and the area.

Meet your clinician

Heidi — Cosmetic Nurse (RN) & owner

Rediscover your confidence.

Skin Cosmetic Clinic is owned and operated by Heidi, a Cosmetic Nurse and Registered Nurse. Every consultation, treatment plan and follow-up is handled by her personally — you see the same clinician each visit, not a rotating roster.

The clinic offers a full menu of skin, injectable and laser treatments across two South Australian locations: the Renmark clinic on Renmark Avenue, and a second location in Parkside/Fullarton, Adelaide.

If you are not sure where to start, book a consultation and Heidi will talk you through what is realistic for your skin.
