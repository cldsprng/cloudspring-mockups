Why this page exists

Right now, one 2020 article is doing the introducing

Search the clinic’s name today and the page that answers is a newspaper round-up of vets open during the April 2020 lockdown. On that single page the business appears twice under two different names — “PetVet Jr. Animal Clinic & Grooming Center” at Talipapa and “PetVet Animal Health Clinic & Grooming Center” at San Bartolome — as though they were two companies. Zabarte is not on it at all. The hours are the lockdown ones. Three other clinics are listed on the same page, one scroll away.

None of that is anyone’s fault; it is simply the only page there is. This one names all three branches, spells the business one way, and belongs to PetVet.

A note on petvet.phThe domain petvet.ph is not the clinic’s — it currently serves a parking router and is not registered to this business. It is mentioned here only so nobody assumes otherwise.
