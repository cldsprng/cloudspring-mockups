Why this page exists

Three websites, three different Sunday schedules — and none of them is hers to correct

Fetched on 16 August 2026 and re-checked on 23 August: clinicfinderph.com publishes Sunday 10:00–12:00 and 1:30–5:00, marks Monday closed, places the clinic in “Taguig BGC”, shows 3 reviews, and offers a “Claim This Clinic” button — which is the page telling you plainly that it is not hers.

seriousmd.com gives Sunday 8:00–11:00 AM for the same doctor. Her own Facebook page says Sunday 10:00 to 5:00. A parent choosing a Sunday to bring in a sick child has three answers and no way to tell which one is current.

The ClinicFinderPH page does one more thing: underneath her listing it lists other pediatricians — Dr. Arnel Salud of St. Luke’s, Zaira Nina De Leon-Duque MD, and The Natural Child Clinic. The page that ranks for her name is also the page recommending her competitors.

Re-checked 23 August 2026: drjewelyncalimbas.com is unregistered. There is no owned page for a correction to live on, which is the whole reason the directories get the last word.
