Why this page exists

Four hundred and seventy-three patients reviewed this clinic. None of that traffic lands on a page it owns.

Search “509 Family Care Clinic Cebu” and the answers are findhealthclinics.com, cebu247.com and Facebook. Re-checked 23 August 2026: 509familycare.com is unregistered. A clinic with 473 Google reviews has no address of its own on the web.

Search the way a patient in Guadalupe actually searches — “family clinic Guadalupe Cebu City” — and back come CentroMed FC, Guadalupe Health & Birthing Center on doktor.ph, Goldenheart on ClinicFinderPH, and Chong Hua on its own domain chonghua.com.ph. 509 is not on that page.

And the appointment rule makes it worse rather than better. findhealthclinics.com records that consultations and vaccines are strictly by appointment, one to three days in advance — and every one of those appointments is currently booked by Facebook message, in a thread, by hand, whenever somebody is at the keyboard.
