The acne program

The Face Reality acne program, step by step

Acne isn't cleared in one facial. The Face Reality approach pairs in-clinic treatments with products matched to your skin, then adjusts both as your skin responds. Here's what working the program with us looks like.

Consult

We look at your skin, talk through your history, products, and what has and hasn't worked for you so far.

Your plan

You leave with a home-care routine matched to your skin type and acne type — not a one-size-fits-all kit.

Treatments

You come in for regular in-clinic acne treatments while your skin adjusts to the routine.

Adjust

At each visit we review your progress and change the routine or treatment as your skin changes.
