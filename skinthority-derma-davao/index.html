About the clinic

Skinthority Dermatology Clinic, Davao City

Skinthority Dermatology Clinic is a dermatology practice on Artiaga St in the Poblacion District of Davao City, offering both medical and cosmetic skin care — from acne management and mole and wart removal to laser treatments and chemical peels.

Consultations are led by Dr. Joanne Kate Milana-Martinez, MD, FPDS, DPACS. Appointments are currently arranged by phone or by message on Facebook and Instagram.
