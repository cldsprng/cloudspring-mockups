Why this page exists

The two pages a customer lands on are the two they can’t tap the number on

Audited 22 August 2026, page by page. On mannandmachinevt.com, 802-434-7054 sits as plain text in an H1 on the homepage reading “Call us today”, and in an H4 on /contact-us/. Neither is a tel: link. Meanwhile /about-us/ and /services/ both have working click-to-call. The two pages nobody starts on work; the two pages everybody starts on do not.

Worth being precise about: this is not “no click-to-call anywhere”, which would be false. Half the site has it. That is what makes it a ten-second fix rather than a rebuild — and it is checkable on your own phone right now.

Then there is the BBB profile ranking for the shop’s name — twelve years accredited, four staff, owner Peter Bormann — which lists no website at all. The page most likely to be seen by someone checking the shop out sends them nowhere. Twenty-eight years of reputation, and the reputation page is a dead end.

One more thing worth knowing: the shop is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 5:00. That is 45 open hours a week, which leaves 123 hours in which a message sent through the site’s contact form sits unread — the whole weekend included.
