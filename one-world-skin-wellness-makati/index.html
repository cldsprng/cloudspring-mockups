About us

One World Skin & Wellness

One World Skin & Wellness is a skin and wellness clinic offering facials, skin treatments and aesthetic services. The clinic operates four branches: Quezon City, Makati, Parañaque and Santa Rosa, Laguna.

Our Makati branch is on the 2nd Floor of the Diane Optical Building along Arnaiz Avenue.

This page brings all four branches together in one place, so you can find the right location, the right treatment and a way to reach us without hunting across separate listings.
