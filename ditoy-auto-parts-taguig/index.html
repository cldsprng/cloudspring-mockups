Why this page exists

The branch that is hardest to find is the one in Metro Manila

Checked 23 August 2026. The Tarlac branch has the stronger footprint of the two. It trades as Ditoy Auto Parts Trading & Services, keeps its own Facebook page at /DAPASTarlac, sits on metroclarkguide.com, and publishes its own landline, +63 45 800 5330.

The Taguig branch trades under a different name, Ditoy Auto Parts and Services, and exists online as a Facebook page and a Waze pin. It does not appear on taguig.com/business/auto-repair/, the free directory page that lists seven Taguig shops. Neither ditoyservices.ph nor ditoyautoparts.com resolves.

So the branch sitting in the largest market is the one a customer is least likely to find, and the two branches never point at each other. Somebody who knows the Tarlac shop and then moves to Manila has no way of discovering there is a Ditoy in Bicutan.

The phone number is not missing, it is just not yours to publishThe Taguig number, 0999 495 6603, together with Mon to Sat 8am to 5pm, is currently published by Waze. A navigation app holds the shop’s contact details while nothing the owner controls does. That is the whole problem in one line.
