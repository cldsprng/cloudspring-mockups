Why this page exists

Three Facebook pages, one dead domain, and five numbers on four networks

Checked 22 August 2026. Search the shop’s own name and Google returns three separate Facebook pages — /GMSautocare01, /gms.autocare, and a third for “GMS Autocare Center & Auto Supply”. A customer who was recommended GMS has to guess which one is the shop.

Google also still indexes gmsautocare.com/contact/. That domain no longer resolves at all: the result is NXDOMAIN, which means a page ranking for this business under “contact” is a dead click. Somebody looking for the number finds an error instead.

And the numbers. The shop’s own site footer carries five, on four different networks: PLDT 082-228-5825, Smart 0908-771-9928, Globe 0956-900-6125, TM 0997-281-7378 and Smart 0949-390-8335. That is a sensible answer to patchy signal in Davao and a confusing one for a first-time customer, who just wants to know which one to ring.

None of this is a criticism of the workshop. It is a naming problem, and a naming problem is fixed with one page that outranks the rest and says plainly which is which.
