About the clinic

ALO Dermatology, Escario Central Mall

ALO Dermatology is a dermatology clinic at Bldg B, Escario Central Mall on N. Escario St, Brgy. Camputhaw, Cebu City. The clinic covers both medical and aesthetic dermatology — from skin condition diagnosis and treatment to dermabrasion, dermaroller and electrotherapy, plus hair and nail concerns.

Consultations are led by Dr. Marie Kristy Alo-Manal, a Diplomate of the Philippine Dermatological Society. Appointments are arranged by phone or through the clinic's Facebook page.
