Why this page exists

The rebrand is done everywhere except the pages that rank

Search “dentist Poblacion District Davao City” and cebudentalimplants.com answers — with the practice filed under its old name, “SmilePH Dental Center Inc”. Search “Dentoq dental Davao” and you get Facebook, Wanderlog, Waze and WhatClinic. Google’s own Website field for the clinic points at m.facebook.com/SmilePHDentalCenter. A patient handed the new name finds the old one.

On the same results page, dentalclinics.care runs pages for the Dental Specialist Center of Davao and for Urbidontics — the latter also ranking on urbidontics.com, its own domain. Five dentists and a perfect 5.0 rating are competing against clinics that own their addresses.

Checked 23 August 2026, and this is the sharp one: dentoq.ph resolves — but not to the practice. It redirects into router.parklogic.com, a domain-parking network that runs adverts on names it holds. The clinic’s new name in the .ph namespace is already being monetised by somebody else, while the clinic itself has no page under it.
