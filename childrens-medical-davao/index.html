Why this page exists

Her top Google result is a profile the site itself says she has never claimed

Search “doktor.ph pediatrician Davao City Redulla children’s medical clinic” and the number one result is her doktor.ph profile — carrying a banner from doktor.ph itself saying the profile is not yet claimed or verified. Beneath it sit a ClinicFinderPH page and a scraper page at ph1010489-dr-mirland-redulla.contact.page. Three pages about her practice; none of them hers.

Search the way a parent searches instead — “pediatrician Agdao Davao City children’s clinic” — and back come doktor.ph and ClinicFinderPH indexes, nowserving.ph, and a SeriousMD profile for Dr. Kate Syvel Rafols-Cruz.
