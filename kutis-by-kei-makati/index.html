About the clinic

Kutis by Kei Dermatology and Skincare Clinic

Kutis by Kei is a dermatology and skincare clinic founded by Dr. Kei George Rebolledo, focused on acne scarring, melasma and pigmentation, and laser dermatology.

The clinic now runs four branches — Parañaque, Mandaluyong, Quezon City and Makati — with a fifth planned in Angeles City. The Makati clinic, in Legazpi Village, opened in 2024 and was redesigned in 2026, when it also took delivery of the Vossman Blauman 450nm Blue Laser.

As of August 2026, patients rate the clinic 5.0★ across 377 Google reviews.
