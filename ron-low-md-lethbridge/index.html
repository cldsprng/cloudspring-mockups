Getting an appointment

How to be seen at this office

Dermatology in Alberta is a specialist service. Here is the usual route from your first concern to your appointment — and who to ask if anything is unclear.

1

See your family doctor

Book with your family physician or nurse practitioner and describe the skin, hair or nail concern you would like assessed.

2

Ask for a referral

Ask them to send a dermatology referral to this office. Referrals are faxed or sent electronically by your provider's clinic.

3

We contact you

Once the referral is received, the office arranges an appointment time with you by phone.

4

Bring your Alberta health card

Bring your Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan card and any referral paperwork to your visit.

Coverage: medically necessary dermatology is generally covered by the Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan when you have a valid Alberta health card and a referral. Cosmetic treatments are not insured services and are paid privately. Please call the office at (403) 328-3100 to confirm how your particular visit is billed before you attend.

About

Dr. Ron Low, MD

Dr. Ron Low is a board-certified dermatologist who has practised in Lethbridge, Alberta since 1987. His office at 1410 Mayor Magrath Drive South provides both medical dermatology — the assessment and management of skin, hair and nail conditions — and cosmetic dermatology treatments.

Medical appointments are arranged through a referral from your family physician or nurse practitioner. Cosmetic treatments such as laser hair removal, laser wrinkle treatment, Botox and chemical peels are booked directly with the office and are paid privately.

If you are unsure which applies to you, call (403) 328-3100 and the office will explain the next step.
