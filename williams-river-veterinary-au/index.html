Why this page exists

A directory nobody at the clinic writes is answering for it

The clinic’s profile on a national veterinary directory is still unclaimed — it carries a “Are you the owner? Claim and manage your business profile” banner to this day. Meanwhile it holds the clinic’s 23 reviews, runs its own enquiry form, carries an Amazon affiliate disclosure, and lists 37 services nobody here wrote.

It also publishes Saturday 9:00–11:30 as machine-readable opening hours — a flat, every-Saturday promise. The clinic’s own site says “open some Saturdays”. Search engines read the machine-readable version. That gap is a drive into Clarence Town for a closed door, and the clinic gets the blame for it.

Two things fix it: a page that says which Saturdays, and a page whose notices are current. The clinic’s homepage still carries Christmas and Easter closure notices from 2023.
